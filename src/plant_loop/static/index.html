How it works

Board loop: cases are ordered by a priority formula in code (safety > line-down > quality > shortage > maintenance, scaled by line criticality and downtime) and worked to a shift budget; the remainder is handed over in writing. Case loop: diagnose (ranked hypotheses, evidence required) → plan (steps, parts checked against the shelf, the SOP's mandatory safety steps non-removable) → verify (a critic round plus code checks; when the shelf cannot satisfy a part, the verify stage searches the KB's compatibility relation in code — a substitute must be in stock and clear the SOP's constraints for the slot — replans around the winner, and records the substitution, or the rejection, on the record). Gaps send the case back for another round — on the gaps only. Exits are decided in code: converged, exhausted, blocked_external (a part only the outside world can supply), safety_stop (safety cases escalate at round 0, always), budget_exceeded. Every case stops at the supervisor gate with a draft work order or escalation note. The agent never actuates anything.
